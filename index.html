Bengal School • Modern India

Known for helping shape a modern Indian visual language rooted in indigenous traditions.
RB

Raja Ravi Varma

Academic & Modern Indian Art

Known for combining European academic techniques with subjects from Indian mythology and culture.

Chola Bronze Tradition

South India • Sculpture

The Chola ateliers became renowned for sophisticated lost-wax bronze images, including Nataraja.
AC

Ajanta Painters

Ancient India • Murals

Anonymous artists created expressive Buddhist narratives and figures across the cave complex.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Art is a record of civilization.

This interactive educational project explores the geographic spread and historical development of Indian art. It combines a map, artifact gallery and timeline so students can connect places, people, art forms and periods.
